How I test AI systems

Systems that don't answer the same way twice

Chatbots and agents fail differently: a wrong answer can sound completely right. I test them with evals — expected behaviors, edge cases and adversarial prompts — and check for hallucinations, regressions between prompt or model versions, and whether the system stays within its limits.

  • Evals
  • Hallucinations
  • Chatbots & agents
